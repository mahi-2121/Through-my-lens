BEHIND THE LENS

More Than Just a Photograph

Have you ever looked at an old photograph and suddenly remembered everything about that moment? Where you were, who was with you, what you were feeling, and even the little details you thought you had forgotten?

That's what photography means to me. It's a way of keeping memories that time would otherwise carry away. I love capturing people in their natural moments, beautiful sunsets, rainy afternoons, trees, coffee, and the little things that make ordinary days special.

Years may pass, places may change, and people may move on, but a photograph gives us something to look back on. It reminds us that those moments happened, and that they will always be part of our story.

A MEMORY WORTH KEEPING

The Sunset That Reminds Me How Far I've Come

I took this photograph about four years ago, during my Grade 12 year. It was a time when life felt uncertain. I was preparing for my university entrance exam, studying long hours, and trying to stay focused despite everything happening around me.

Whenever I needed a break, I would go for a walk around noon to get some fresh air. During one of those days, I captured this beautiful sunset after the rain.

Looking at this picture today takes me back to those days. It reminds me of the worries I had, the walks I took, the dreams I held on to, and how much I've grown since then.

Sometimes, a photograph is more than a beautiful image. It's a reminder of a chapter of our lives that we never want to forget.
